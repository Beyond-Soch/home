Google Business Profile Social Links: A Checklist for Local Brands

By Beyond Soch Editorial · 26 October 2026

What the feature actually does

Google says eligible Business Profiles can show social media links so customers can learn more about a business. The feature is available only in select regions and may not appear for every profile. Where available, Google currently supports one link per listed platform, including Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, TikTok, X and YouTube. These links are contact and discovery signals, not a substitute for a complete profile. Accurate category, address or service area, website and business information still need their own maintenance.

Use the canonical profile URL

Open each social account in a browser and copy its public profile URL, not a post, Reel, login page, tracking redirect or link-shortener. Google provides expected URL patterns for supported platforms. Check spelling, punctuation and whether a renamed account redirects correctly. For LinkedIn, distinguish a company page from an individual profile. A local brand should link the official business identity unless the public-facing business is deliberately built around a named creator. Never add a third party’s page merely because it mentions the business.

Add links through the profile editor

Google’s documented path is to open the Business Profile, select Edit profile, go to Contact, then edit Social profiles. Choose the platform, enter the web address and save. The exact layout can change, so use the live interface and current help article rather than an old screenshot. If the option is missing, do not create a duplicate Business Profile to force it. Confirm that the profile is verified and eligible, then use official support routes if necessary.

Make identity consistent before expecting trust

Use the same business name, logo family, website domain and location cues across the Google profile and social accounts wherever accurate. Fill the social bio with a clear description and working website link. Pin or feature content that helps a new customer understand the offer quickly. Consistency does not mean stuffing the same keyword everywhere; it means removing contradictions. A restaurant with different phone numbers or an old address across profiles creates doubt even when every social link technically works.

Audit what customers can actually see

After saving, test the Business Profile while signed out or from another device. Open every visible social icon and confirm it reaches the intended public account. Google notes that links can also be added automatically in some cases and may be replaced through the profile editor where the option is available. Keep a dated record of the URLs you submitted. Recheck after a username change, platform migration or rebrand, because a formerly correct URL can later redirect or fail.

Measure carefully and keep expectations realistic

Google’s help page says it does not currently provide performance metrics or click tracking specifically for social links. Use platform insights, website analytics with appropriate campaign URLs where allowed, and enquiry questions to understand impact—but do not attribute every new follower to the profile. Review links monthly alongside hours, photos and services. For Beyond Soch collaborations, give a local business one clean action: visit the profile, view recent work or send an enquiry. Clear paths outperform a cluster of broken or duplicated links.
